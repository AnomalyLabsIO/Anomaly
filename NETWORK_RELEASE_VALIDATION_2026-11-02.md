## Upload and admission policy

Server defaults: `bulk_client_bytes_per_second` 8 MiB/s, `bulk_upload_bytes_per_second` 16 MiB/s, `bulk_gameplay_headroom_percent` 20. The rate settings are accounted allowances **before** reserve, not measured wire rates. Each chunk costs payload +25% +256 bytes; a 16 KiB chunk costs 20,736 bytes. The ideal default payload allowance is about **5.06 MiB/s per client**, before scheduling/network/storage effects. Operators must budget against their actual uplink; capacity is not detected automatically.

The pump admits at most 512 KiB/32 frames per update and checks 2 ms between operations. One operation, maintenance or GC can exceed 2 ms. Admission uses bounded FIFO capacity, at most eight active/32 queued transfers and 512 MiB requested-content reservation, with verification/activation reserve inside the remaining fixed 120-second content deadline. [Scheduler CPU forecasting](E:/Projects/AnomalyLabs/Anomaly/Anomaly.Server/FileTransfer/BulkSchedulerCapacity.cs:67) caps that plan by measured ready-send service/cadence capacity; it does not raise the 2 ms allowance or infer CPU capacity from ACK/credit/storage waits. These are policies/estimates, not guarantees of player latency or voice quality.
